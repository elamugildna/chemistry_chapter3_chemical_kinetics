Class 12 Chapter 1: Sexual Reproduction in Flowering Plants

1. Rate of Reaction, Rate Law & Order vs. Molecularity

Rate of Reaction & Stoichiometry

• For reaction $aA + bB \rightarrow cC + dD$:
$$\text{Rate} = -\frac{1}{a}\frac{d[A]}{dt} = -\frac{1}{b}\frac{d[B]}{dt} = +\frac{1}{c}\frac{d[C]}{dt} = +\frac{1}{d}\frac{d[D]}{dt}$$
• Units of Rate: $\text{mol L}^{-1}\text{ s}^{-1}$ (or $\text{atm s}^{-1}$ for gases).
• Average vs. Instantaneous Rate: Average rate is calculated over large time interval ($\Delta[R]/\Delta t$); instantaneous rate is the tangent slope at time $t$ ($-d[R]/dt$).

Order vs. Molecularity

• Rate Law: $\text{Rate} = k[A]^x [B]^y$ (Exponents $x, y$ are determined experimentally).
• Order ($n = x + y$): Can be 0, 1, 2, 3, or fractional; applies to elementary and complex reactions.
• Molecularity: Number of reacting species colliding simultaneously in an elementary step; always integer (1, 2, or 3), never zero or fractional.
• Units of $k$: $(\text{mol L}^{-1})^{1-n} \text{ s}^{-1}$ (Zero order: $\text{mol L}^{-1}\text{ s}^{-1}$; First order: $\text{s}^{-1}$; Second order: $\text{L mol}^{-1}\text{ s}^{-1}$).

2. Integrated Rate Equations & Half-Life Formulas

Zero-Order Kinetics ($R \rightarrow P$)

• Differential Equation: $-d[R]/dt = k$
• Integrated Equation: $[R] = [R]_0 - kt$ (Plot of $[R]$ vs $t$ gives slope $= -k$, intercept $= [R]_0$)
• Rate Constant: $k = \frac{[R]_0 - [R]}{t}$
• Half-life ($t_{1/2}$): $t_{1/2} = \frac{[R]_0}{2k}$ ($t_{1/2} \propto [R]_0$)
• Time for 100% completion: $t_{100\%} = \frac{[R]_0}{k} = 2 \times t_{1/2}$
• Examples: Decomposition of $\text{NH}_3$ on hot Pt at high pressure; decomposition of $\text{HI}$ on gold.

First-Order Kinetics ($R \rightarrow P$)

• Integrated Equation: $k = \frac{2.303}{t}\log\frac{[R]_0}{[R]}$ or $[R] = [R]_0 e^{-kt}$
• Linear Plots: $\ln[R]$ vs $t$ (slope $= -k$); $\log\frac{[R]_0}{[R]}$ vs $t$ (slope $= \frac{k}{2.303}$)
• Half-life ($t_{1/2}$): $t_{1/2} = \frac{0.693}{k}$ (Independent of $[R]_0$)
• High-yield Relationships:
- $t_{75\%} = 2 \times t_{1/2}$
- $t_{87.5\%} = 3 \times t_{1/2}$
- $t_{99.9\%} = 10 \times t_{1/2}$ • $t_{99\%} = 2 \times t_{90\%}$
• Gas Phase: $k = \frac{2.303}{t}\log\frac{p_i}{2p_i - p_t}$ for $A(g) \rightarrow B(g) + C(g)$.

3. Temperature Dependence, Arrhenius Equation & Collision Theory

Arrhenius Equation

• $k = A e^{-E_a/RT}$
• $\ln k = \ln A - \frac{E_a}{RT}$ (slope $= -E_a/R$)
• $\log k = \log A - \frac{E_a}{2.303 RT}$ (slope $= -\frac{E_a}{2.303 R}$)
• Two-Temperature Formula:
$$\log\frac{k_2}{k_1} = \frac{E_a}{2.303 R}\left[\frac{T_2 - T_1}{T_1 T_2}\right]$$
• Temperature Coefficient: $\frac{k_{T+10}}{k_T} \approx 2\text{ to }3$.

Effect of Catalyst

• Provides an alternate reaction path with lower activation energy ($E_a$).
• Accelerates both forward and reverse reaction rates equally.
• Reaches equilibrium faster without changing $K_{eq}$, $\Delta H$, or $\Delta G$.
• $\Delta H = E_{a(f)} - E_{a(b)}$: Exothermic when $E_{a(f)} < E_{a(b)}$; Endothermic when $E_{a(f)} > E_{a(b)}$.

Collision Theory

• Rate Equation: $\text{Rate} = P Z_{AB} e^{-E_a/RT}$
• $Z_{AB}$: Collision frequency of reactants.
• $e^{-E_a/RT}$: Fraction of molecules with energy $\ge E_a$.
• $P$: Steric / probability factor ensuring proper molecular orientation.
• Threshold Energy $= E_a + \text{Average energy of reactants}$.
